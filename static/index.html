Roles and resumes

Each role has its own job titles and its own master resume. Upajna searches every role that has a resume, and tailors each job from the matching one. Tailoring only trims and rewords what's in that resume. It never invents experience.

Send jobs to Upajna

Upajna searches job sites that allow it on its own. For LinkedIn, send it the jobs you find. It reads that one public job page and never signs in to your account.
